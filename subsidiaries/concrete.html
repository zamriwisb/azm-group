About the Company

Strength Built into Every Product

AZM Concrete Industries Sdn Bhd is a subsidiary under AZM Global Solution Sdn Bhd within AZM Group. Previously, its business activities were managed under the Construction Division of AZM Global Solution Sdn Bhd. To strengthen management and operational efficiency, AZM Concrete Industries Sdn Bhd was officially established on 12 January 2026.

The company is a 100% Bumiputera company based in Kelantan, specializing in the manufacturing and supply of concrete products such as concrete barriers, U-drains, box culverts, road kerbs, and retaining walls.

With dedicated manufacturing facilities and a commitment to precision, AZM Concrete Industries delivers reliable concrete products that form the foundation of infrastructure projects across the region.
