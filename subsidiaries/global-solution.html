About the Company

The Foundation of AZM Group

AZM Global Solution Sdn Bhd is the core operating company under AZM Group, established on 26 February 2018, serving as the foundation for multiple business divisions across construction, safety, machinery, and services.

The company is registered with the Ministry of Finance Malaysia (MOF) and certified by CIDB Malaysia as a Grade G4 contractor. Through its diversified divisions, the company delivers integrated solutions with a focus on quality, reliability, and efficiency.

AZM Global Solution serves as the operational backbone of the group, housing key business divisions that span scaffolding, machinery, safety equipment, signage fabrication, and pest control services.
