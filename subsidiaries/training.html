About the Company

Empowering Teams Through Knowledge

AZM Training & Consultancy Sdn Bhd is a subsidiary under AZM Global Solution Sdn Bhd, focusing on Occupational Safety and Health (OSH) training and industry consultancy services. Training activities were initially conducted under AZM Global Solution Sdn Bhd since 2018 through its training division.

Due to growing commitment and demand as a training provider, AZM Training & Consultancy Sdn Bhd was officially incorporated on 21 June 2019. The company specializes in delivering OSH training programs, including both public and in-house sessions, as well as providing consultancy services for various industries.

AZM Training & Consultancy complements the role of AZM Global Solution Sdn Bhd, which continues to focus on the supply of safety equipment and Personal Protective Equipment (PPE). Together, they provide a comprehensive safety ecosystem — from training and awareness to equipment and compliance.
