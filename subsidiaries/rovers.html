About the Company

More Than Just Football

Red Rovers Sdn Bhd was established to manage AZM Rovers FC, overseeing the club's development from academy level to the highest level of professional football.

In addition to CSR contributions from companies under AZM Group, Red Rovers Sdn Bhd has also undertaken various revenue-generating initiatives to support and sustain the club's football operations.

AZM Rovers Football Club

AZM Rovers FC is a football club under the auspices of AZM Group, established to elevate the standard of local football. Formed in 2019 and officially registered in 2024, the club actively participates in competitions while focusing on youth development and community programs.
