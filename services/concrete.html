Concrete & Manufacturing

Quality concrete products manufactured to specification and delivered on schedule.

What We Do

Strength Built into Every Product

AZM Concrete Industries Sdn Bhd is a subsidiary under AZM Global Solution Sdn Bhd within AZM Group. Previously, its business activities were managed under the Construction Division of AZM Global Solution Sdn Bhd. To strengthen management and operational efficiency, AZM Concrete Industries Sdn Bhd was officially established on 12 January 2026.

The company is a 100% Bumiputera company based in Kelantan, specializing in the manufacturing and supply of concrete products such as concrete barriers, U-drains, box culverts, road kerbs, and retaining walls. Every product is manufactured to meet stringent quality and safety standards.

With dedicated manufacturing facilities and a commitment to precision, AZM Concrete Industries delivers reliable concrete products that form the foundation of infrastructure projects across the region.

Concrete Barriers

Durable concrete barriers manufactured to specification for road safety and site protection.

U-Drains & Box Culverts

Precast U-drains and box culverts for drainage and water management infrastructure projects.

Road Kerbs

High-quality road kerbs manufactured to standard dimensions for road and infrastructure works.

Retaining Walls

Precast retaining wall systems designed for slope stabilization and earth retention projects.
