Safety & Signage

Keeping workers protected and sites compliant with certified safety solutions.

What We Do

Protection & Compliance at Every Level

AZM Safetyware is a division under AZM Global Solution Sdn Bhd, specializing in the supply and distribution of safety equipment for construction sites, roadwork, and industrial use. Our comprehensive range covers Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), safety equipment for construction and industrial use, and temporary traffic control equipment.

AZM Signboard & Fabrication is another key division under AZM Global Solution Sdn Bhd, focusing on the supply, fabrication, and installation of road signage and related structures. We provide reliable and compliant solutions for road safety and infrastructure projects, including supply and installation of road signboards, fabrication of traffic signage structures, and road traffic accessories.

Together, these two divisions deliver a complete safety and signage solution — from PPE and site equipment to certified road signage and fabrication works — keeping workers protected and sites fully compliant.

PPE Supply

Hard hats, safety boots, gloves, harnesses, goggles, and full-body protection equipment.

Construction Safety Equipment

Safety equipment for construction and industrial use, including barriers, nets, and site essentials.

Road Signboard & Fabrication

Supply, fabrication, and installation of road signboards and traffic signage structures.

Traffic Control Equipment

Temporary traffic control equipment, road traffic accessories, and installation works for roadwork sites.
