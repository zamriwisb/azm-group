What We Do

Built on Trust

AZM Bina Gemilang Sdn Bhd is a subsidiary of AZM Global Solution Sdn Bhd under AZM Group. Established on 31st May 2021, the company focuses on strengthening construction management and operations. We are a 100% Bumiputera company based in Kelantan and registered with the Construction Industry Development Board (CIDB) Malaysia as a Grade G3 contractor.

We specialize in helping clients build their dream homes on their own land, providing a complete solution from design, planning, approval, to construction. We uphold trust as the foundation of our business — building strong relationships with clients, subcontractors, suppliers, and all stakeholders through honest and transparent communication.

We take full responsibility to deliver projects on time, with quality and in compliance with specifications. We are open to feedback and continuously improving, while creating a safe, harmonious, and professional working environment for all parties.
