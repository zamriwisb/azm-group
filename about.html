Our Story

Who We Are

AZM Group is a one-stop construction and industrial business centre headquartered in Kelantan, Malaysia. What started as a focused venture in scaffolding supply and construction services has grown into a diversified group of companies operating across multiple sectors of the construction and industrial industry.

With its core operating company, AZM Global Solution Sdn Bhd, established on 26 February 2018, the group has steadily expanded its capabilities — earning MOF registration and CIDB Grade G4 certification along the way. Today, the group encompasses subsidiaries specializing in construction management, training & consultancy, concrete manufacturing, and even sports development.

Through its diversified divisions and subsidiaries, AZM Group delivers integrated solutions with a focus on quality, reliability, and efficiency. From scaffolding and heavy machinery to safety equipment, OSH training, road signage, pest control, and concrete products — the group serves clients across government, corporate, and private sectors.

Guided by a commitment to excellence and driven by strong values, AZM Group continues to grow — building value, delivering trust, and empowering growth across every project and partnership.
